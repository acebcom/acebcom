## What is ACEB?

[ACEB](https://aceb.com) is an online shop where you **buy digital gift cards with cryptocurrency**. Pay with Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDC and other popular coins — and receive your gift card code by email, usually within minutes of payment.

> **No account. No hidden fees. No exchange in between.**

Whether you want to shop on Amazon, stream Netflix, top up Steam, order on Uber Eats, or book a stay on Airbnb — [ACEB](https://aceb.com) turns your crypto into real-world spending power.

## 🤖 Shop with an AI Assistant

ACEB runs a free public **MCP server** for AI assistants — no account, no API key:

```
https://api.aceb.com/mcp
```

Add it to ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or any other MCP client and ask *"find a 50 EUR Steam card for Germany"*. The assistant searches the catalog, builds a cart and hands you a link. You always pay yourself, and gift card codes only ever arrive by email.
